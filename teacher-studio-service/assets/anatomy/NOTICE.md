# heart-v1 anatomy template

`heart-v1.glb` is a teaching asset derived from Z-Anatomy / Z-Anatomy-Sample.

The chamber volumes and septa marked with `teachingApproximation=true` are
visual teaching aids generated from the source chamber bounds. They are not
intended for measurement, diagnosis, treatment planning, or clinical use.
